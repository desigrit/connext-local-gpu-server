Shambu pen-sketch icon.

The master shambu.png is the unmodified approved concept from docs/concepts/shambu-stick-sketch.png, generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool from the archived Shikari Shambu portrait. The full prompt is in docs/concepts/shambu-stick-sketch-prompt.txt. Platform icons are Tauri CLI exports of this master.
