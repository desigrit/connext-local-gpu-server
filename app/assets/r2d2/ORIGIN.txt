Origin: Shambu's final icon combines a detailed right-facing R2-D2 vector drawing with the preserved forest from the earlier Shambu binoculars illustration.
Droid: app/assets/r2d2/r2d2-droid.svg, drawn in editable SVG using the project owner's supplied flat blue, silver, and white R2-D2 image as a visual reference. The source reference is preserved unchanged at app/assets/archive/r2d2-reference/r2d2-flat-reference.png, SHA-256 485D2AAC6577674AADC4CC2AA70C1A3C533BC4E96355A8B41453FFE67DCE229F. Its original artist and source URL were not supplied. The droid faces right in three-quarter view, has projecting dome sensors and distinct body panels, and fits entirely within the circle. The existing vector is scaled 25 percent larger and raised within the circle to make the droid the primary focus. It sits ahead of the original upper bushes, with the lowest original leaves overlapping its base. This refinement changes only vector transforms and layer ordering; the droid drawing and forest source images are unchanged. It is not an official Star Wars illustration and does not use the retired LibreICONS glyph.
Original forest: app/assets/archive/shikari-shambu/shambu-binoculars.png, SHA-256 CC3CD7D6394F6FCF81F407E7F3B80DD120DB78BDB36483ABA69648C257F7C244. The unchanged source PNG is embedded in the composition. The original visible canopy outside the former subject footprint and clipped foreground leaf contours are reused directly, with uniform scaling for export.
Occluded-area repair: app/assets/r2d2/forest-subject-fill.png, SHA-256 22E21505414770D34D5CF5ED2E6B96C0C7F2D18D417E8F34D3F4F77AD737E874. This was made with the built-in image generation tool by removing the old man and binoculars and continuing the forest in the formerly hidden area. Its exact prompt is app/assets/r2d2/forest-subject-fill.prompt.txt. The final SVG clips this repair to the old subject footprint. It is not used as a replacement for the visible original forest.
The original forest source is an earlier AI-edited Shikari Shambu illustration based on supplied imagery. Its supplied jungle reference, original ACK reference illustrations, and exact edit prompts are retained in app/assets/archive/shikari-shambu. Shikari Shambu was created by Luis Fernandes and Vasant Halbe for Tinkle, published by Amar Chitra Katha Pvt. Ltd. Publisher reference: https://www.issuewire.com/tinkles-shikari-shambu-turns-38-1700939605829767 .
